# Structure Add-on for Horizon

A data-only Horizon mod (`structure_addon`) that brings several old structure and world-generation mods into one place:

- **Underground Biomes Constructs:** 56 stones (granites, marbles, basalts, shales and more) as big strata. Most of the world stays plain stone: elsewhere each stone appears in about one chunk in 64. Stone biomes (Stony, Jagged and Frozen Peaks, windswept hills, stony shores, dripstone caves, and Biomes O' Plenty's crags, volcanoes, highlands and the like) are full of them, from just under the surface down into the mountain.
- **Recurrent Complex:** its surface buildings (towers, pyramids, forts, huts and more) and its underground crypts.
- **Ancient Structures:** German, Japanese and Mayan buildings, villages and temples.
- **Generatormods:** its building templates, Great Walls, walled cities with streets, and cellular-automaton ruins on the surface, plus underground cities and dungeons.
- **Wild Caves:** cave decoration (stalactites, stalagmites, icicles, moss) and miner camps.

Surface structures are very rare: each set spaces its structures 256 to 448 blocks apart, and only one place in 60 of those gets one. Underground ones are more common, though Generatormods' dungeons spawn in at most half the places 48 chunks apart, and its tall maze dungeon is always deep underground (it used to stick out of low ground as a sandstone-coloured tower).

## Install

Download `structure_addon-<version>.hmod` from the [releases](https://github.com/mxpbxb/structure-addon/releases), or install it from the Horizon mod registry, then press Build in the launcher.

## How it is made

`content/` is generated by `tools/gen.py` from the original mods' jars (not included here). `tools/gm_proc.py` turns Generatormods' templates and generators into Horizon structures, `tools/blocks.py` maps old block ids to modern block states, and `tools/nbt.py` reads and writes NBT. The script expects this layout under `~/horizon-dev/structures-port`:

```
jars/ubc/UndergroundBiomesConstructs-1.7.10-0.8-beta49.jar
jars/rc/RecurrentComplex-0.9.7.1.2.jar
jars/as/ancientstructures-fabric-1.20.1-0.0.16.jar
jars/wc/wildcaves-1.2.1.jar
jars/gm/ (Generatormods jar and its template config)
vanilla263/ (the game's data, for the biome list)
```

a Horizon checkout at `~/horizon-dev/forks/horizon` for its block-state table, and the game's mod-content cache for its block facts (set `HORIZON_CACHE` to the `.horizon-cache` folder; under WSL it is found on its own). Build the mod with `cargo horizon-mod build` in `content/`.

The Great Wall, walled city and cellular-automaton ruin generators are re-implemented from Generatormods' Java source (LGPL-3.0), which is not included here.

Some things are not ported because the originals build them in code: Recurrent Complex's mazes and some village pieces, Generatormods' Nether cities, and one corrupt Ancient Structures file. Stairs on wall walkways are left out, as Horizon's walls are built from fixed pieces.
